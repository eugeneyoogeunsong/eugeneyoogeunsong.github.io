Attended the [Royal Astronomical Society](https://ras.ac.uk/) Early Career Researchers meeting, organised by former UCL colleague [Ruth Kelly](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ruth-kelly-02367b214/), with a careers panel spanning academia, industry, teaching and public engagement. The highlight was finally meeting [Dr Robert Massey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/robertmassey1969/), Deputy Executive Director of the RAS: back in 2018 he gave Korean broadcaster SBS a television interview explaining my [first-author _MNRAS Letters_ paper](https://academic.oup.com/mnrasl/article/471/1/L135/4036899) on enhanced gamma radiation from extremely rotating black holes, published when I was 19.
